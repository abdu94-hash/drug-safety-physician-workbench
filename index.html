Before you begin

How this workbench works

  • Education only. These tools support the exercises in the book. They are not medical, legal, immigration, or career-placement advice.
  • No credential. Practice scores are self-assessed against a learning rubric. They are not a validated employment test, and no certificate is issued.
  • Not PV experience. Completing these cases is preparation, not pharmacovigilance employment. Describe it honestly.
  • Private by design. Nothing is sent anywhere. Your answers are saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases them, so use Back up my work to keep a copy.
  • Fictional cases. All patients, products, and companies in the cases are fictional. Do not enter real patient or employer information.
