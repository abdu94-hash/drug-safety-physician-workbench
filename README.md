# Drug Safety Physician Workbench

Free companion workbench for *Becoming a Drug Safety Physician: A Practical Guide to Entering and Advancing in Pharmacovigilance* by Hafez Selim, MD, PhD (Selim Medical Press, 2026).

**Live site:** https://abdu94-hash.github.io/drug-safety-physician-workbench/

## What it contains
- **Practice cases** — the eight full fictional cases from Part 3 (Chapters 13–18). Information is released in stages; you write your assessment at *Pause and assess*, then compare it with the model answer and score yourself on the book's six-domain, 0–3 rubric with the critical-error check.
- **Resume builder (ATS-friendly)** — one-column resume or international CV with standard headings; Word (.docx) and plain-text downloads built in the browser; job-ad term match and Worksheet 20.1; honesty checks based on Chapter 20; cover letter and LinkedIn drafts. "ATS-friendly" describes the format, not a guarantee that any tracking system will pass an application.
- **Career planners** — interactive versions of the book's worksheets: diagnostic and reading path (0.1), starting profile and eligibility (2.1–2.2), entry-route scorer (4.1), bridge-role check (4.2, 21.2), training selection scorecard (5.1), 12-week plan (6.1), application tracker (21.1), and search troubleshooter (Figure 22.1).

## Privacy
No accounts, analytics, or server. Everything you type is stored only in your browser's local storage. Use *Back up my work* to save a copy.

## Terms
Educational use only. Not medical, legal, immigration, or employment advice. Practice scores are self-assessed against a learning rubric; they are not a validated employment test or a credential, and no certificate is issued. Educational exercises are not pharmacovigilance experience. All cases, products, and companies are fictional.
